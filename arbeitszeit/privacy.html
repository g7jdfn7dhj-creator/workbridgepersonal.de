Privacy Policy

Working Hours Management (WHM)
Publisher: VitaliiBielykh
Last updated: 4 September 2026

1. Summary

Working Hours Management is a timesheet, staff journal, and invoicing app. It is designed to store your data on your device (browser / PWA local storage). We do not operate a central user account database for this app.

2. Data stored on your device

Depending on what you enter, the app may store locally:

  • project and timesheet entries (dates, hours, sites, notes);
  • employee / staff names and related journal fields;
  • company and invoice details you type (addresses, tax IDs, bank data, counterparties);
  • app settings such as language and draft form values;
  • optional signature images you draw in the app.

This data stays on the device unless you export, share, print, or back it up.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not require you to create an online account to use the core offline features.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the app content for advertising profiling.

4. Network use

When online, the app may load its own files (HTML, scripts, icons, updates) from the hosting URL you installed from. Microsoft Store / Windows packaging may also contact Microsoft services as part of normal Store app behaviour.

5. Microsoft Store

If you install Working Hours Management from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process diagnostic and Store-related information under Microsoft’s own policies.

6. Your choices

You can clear app data by clearing site data / uninstalling the app, or by using in-app delete actions where available. Exported PDF/CSV/backup files are under your control.
